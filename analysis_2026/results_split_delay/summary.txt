SPLIT MODE, SECOND-TRANCHE DELAY SWEEP (pre-registration: analysis_2026/SPLIT_DELAY_PREREG.md)
Tranche 1: 50% at N=1. Tranche 2: 50% at (n_entry, n_exit), n_entry in [1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12], n_exit in [2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 20]. Benchmark split(5,5).
Sharpe = annualised Sharpe of daily returns above T-bills. Costs per side: stocks/ETFs 0.03%, crypto 0.15%.
CAGR_tax_%: strategies 35% on each calendar year's growth, losses carried (flip_delay.stats); buy & hold 20% once at the end.
trades_per_yr: orders per member per year (each weight change counts once, a 0.5 split step included).
Choices the pre-registration leaves open (fixed before the first full run; see the module docstring):
  P2 candidates need 252 daily returns before 1 January and a close in the 7 days before it; top ceil(n/5) held.
  Reality check statistic: max over the 36 variants of (Sharpe - Sharpe split(5,5)), recentred, common indices.
  ETFs pay the stock cost (0.03%); crypto pays 0.15% for every coin incl. BTC/ETH (earlier studies: 0.10%).
  A member-year needs more than 20 bars up to 2026-09-30. OOS halves = first / second half of OOS days.
  Seeds: OOS tests 1/2/3, reality checks 11/12/13, secondary 21-25; reported-only: reality-check seed sensitivity 111-115/121-125/131-135, exploratory split(5,8) test 31-33.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS OF THE INHERITED UNIVERSE (identical for all 36 variants; audit counts)
  - quality_filter(keep_collapsed=True) judges each whole series, later data included: a series that collapses later is cut 20 bars
    after its first fall below 10% of its running peak, which can drop earlier member-years. 33 S&P member-years (9 names, e.g. CHK
    2007-2018, GNW 2009-2015, DYN 2003-2009) are lost this way, and these names cannot enter P2 in those years.
  - A member-year needs more than 20 bars in the year, which uses data after 1 January: about 20 P1 member-years (about 10 in P2) of
    early-year acquisitions/delistings are excluded (e.g. MERQ, RBK, SEBL, BRCM, STJ, CXO).
  - altcoins.tiers_for_year needs a bar in 1-4 January; with data cut at 1 January + 3 days the crypto membership is identical in all 8 years.
  - Together about 0.2-0.3% of member-years: they move absolute basket levels slightly, not the differences between variants.
