How to use it (manual orders only):
  1. Before your first routine, place one small bid at the deepest rung and confirm that Robinhood accepts it and
      shows it as pending. For example: BUY 0.01528 AAVE at limit 65.47 (about $1.00, at least Robinhood's minimum
      size; the k = 10 rung). Cancel it after the check.
  2. In the app: coin -> Trade -> Buy -> order type Limit; enter the sheet's limit price and quantity (several orders
      where a rung is split). Crypto limit orders rest up to 90 days on Robinhood.
  3. Each week (after Monday 03:30 UTC: Sunday 10:30 pm EST / 11:30 pm EDT): run the sheet with your app's mids
      (--price), cancel last week's unfilled bids and enter the new ones (the order diff below lists them).
  4. After a fill: enter the flip target as a limit sell and sell at market after 72 h if it has not filled.
  5. Keep enough cash for every bid at once: fills come on the same crash day across coins.
