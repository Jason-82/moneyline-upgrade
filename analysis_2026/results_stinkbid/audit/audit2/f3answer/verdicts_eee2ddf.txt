Printed with every answer:
- A bid's discount is not a gain by itself. If prices are unpredictable, a bid 30% below the price buys the same expected value as buying now, minus what the coin earns while you wait; in a crash that gaps through your price, it buys less. Only a quick rebound after the wick can pay for the wait.
- Bids fill together. On the worst day, 95% of the live bids in the study's main universe filled the same day. Size the whole ladder as one position you are willing to hold through a further 50-80% fall. [day 2025-10-10: 94 of 99 live P1 bids on U-Q1]
- A fill is not a bottom: 27.3% of past fills were 50% or more below the fill price a year later, against 28.4% for random buys of the same coins. [1329 P1 fills with a year observed]
- Coin-specific collapses (LUNA, FTT, OM) look like ordinary wicks on the first day; a resting order cannot read the news. Cancel a coin's bids on news about the coin itself (a hack, exploit, depeg, delisting or regulatory action).
- Most past fills came when the coin's MoneyLine was already red; a fill buys against your own trend signal. Decide before the fill which rule governs the position.
